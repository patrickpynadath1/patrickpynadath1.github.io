# Homepage redesign

The homepage uses static outlined circles, cool white (#f4f7fc), pale blue panels (#e8f0fc), and dark slate text (#23324b). Source Serif 4 headings pair with Noto Sans body text. The portrait and social links are centered; paper links have subtle teal, rose, and slate backgrounds. Anime.js animates native details panels and respects reduced motion.

Original biography, photographs, cats, research data, and article text are preserved. The welcome banner was removed at the user's request. Blog pages retain their original dark theme and sans-serif typography. Long contact links wrap on phones, while wide equations scroll within the article.
